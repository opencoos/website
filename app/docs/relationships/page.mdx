# Entity relationships

Entities refer to each other by identifier. A field such as `strataLotId` holds the `id` of a `StrataLot`; a field such as `documentIds` holds a list of identifiers.

## Principles

- **Reference, don't embed.** Records refer to other records by identifier rather than nesting them, so each record can be exchanged and updated independently.
- **The strata corporation is the anchor.** Most records carry a `strataCorporationId`, which makes it easy to partition data by corporation.
- **Child-to-parent direction.** References point from the more specific record to the more general one (a `CouncilMember` refers to its `StrataCouncil`, not the other way round).
- **Polymorphic references are explicit.** Where a field can refer to more than one kind of entity, a companion field says which — for example `Resolution.meetingType` with `meetingId`, and `OwnershipInterest.partyType` with `partyId`.

## Core shape

```text
StrataCorporation ─┬─ StrataPlan
                   ├─ Building ── StrataLot ── Unit
                   │               └─ OwnershipInterest ── Person | Organization
                   ├─ StrataCouncil ── CouncilMember ── Person
                   ├─ GeneralMeeting / CouncilMeeting ── Resolution ── VotingRecord
                   ├─ Fund ── FinancialTransaction / Budget / Levy
                   ├─ CommonAsset ── MaintenanceRequest ── WorkOrder
                   └─ Document / InsurancePolicy
```
